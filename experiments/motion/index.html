Motion studies.

A visual dictionary for things that move.

Reduced motion is on. Hover previews are paused; click or press Enter to play an example once.

Timing

Linear

Steady speed from start to finish. No acceleration, no deceleration.

Timing

Ease in

Starts gently, then accelerates as it moves away.

Timing

Ease out

Moves quickly at first, then slows into its destination.

Timing

Ease in-out

Accelerates out of rest and decelerates back into rest.

Physics

Anticipation

A small move in the opposite direction prepares the main action.

Physics

Overshoot

Travels a little beyond the destination, then corrects itself.

Physics

Spring

Oscillates around the destination, losing energy with each pass.

Physics

Bounce

Hits a boundary and rebounds, with each rebound getting smaller.

Physics

Squash & stretch

Changes shape with speed and impact while roughly preserving area.

Choreography

Stagger

The same action starts at slightly different times for each object.

Choreography

Follow-through

The leading object stops first; the others keep moving before settling.

Choreography

Arc

Follows a curved path instead of a straight line.

Choreography

Orbit

Moves around a shared center while maintaining a relationship to it.

Transitions

Fade

Changes visibility without changing position or size.

Transitions

Scale

Grows from a small point into its full size.

Transitions

Masked reveal

Moves through a fixed clipping boundary into view.

Transitions

Crossfade

One object fades away while another appears in the same place.

Transitions

Shared element

An object keeps its identity as it moves and resizes between contexts.

Scroll

Parallax

Layers travel at different rates as the viewport moves, creating a sense of depth.

Scroll

Scroll scrubbing

Scroll position sets the animation’s progress. Reverse the scroll and the motion reverses too.

Scroll

Pinning

One element holds its position while the surrounding content continues past it.

Scroll

Horizontal sequence

Vertical scroll progress moves a sequence sideways inside a stationary viewport.

Scroll

View reveal

An element’s position within the viewport controls how much of it is visible.

Scroll

Stacking

Successive layers arrive over earlier ones, which recede but remain visible.

Spatial

Spatial

Depth travel

Objects move toward the viewer through space; closer objects grow more quickly.

Spatial

3D carousel

Objects rotate around a common axis, passing in front of and behind one another.

Spatial

Exploded layers

Overlapping parts separate in space while keeping their alignment.

Spatial

Particle field

Spatial

Perspective tilt

A plane tilts around two axes so its circles become foreshortened.

Orbit shot

Camera

Camera
