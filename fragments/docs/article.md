Open with a hook. A scene, a question, a strong opinion — something that earns the reader's attention before you get into the substance. Keep it tight; one paragraph is plenty.

Set up the tension or the idea you're going to explore. What did you notice, struggle with, or read recently that made you want to write this? Link out to anything you're reacting to, like [this thing I read](https://example.com).

Land on the core question or claim of the piece. Make it explicit so the rest of the post has something to build on.

##First Point
Make the point in the first sentence. Spend the rest of the paragraph backing it up — a story, an example, a counterargument you considered. Keep it conversational.

##Second Point
Same shape. Lead with the claim, support with detail. If you're tempted to add a third paragraph here, ask whether it belongs in its own section.

##Third Point
Three sections feels about right for most posts. Four is fine. Five and you should consider whether there's a tighter version.

Close with a takeaway. What should the reader walk away thinking, doing, or reconsidering? A single line is often enough — resist the urge to summarize everything you just said.
